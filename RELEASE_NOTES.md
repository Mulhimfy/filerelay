Install: download **FileRelay.apk** below on your phone and open it (allow "install unknown apps" for your browser if asked).

- Share any file to FileRelay and send it to Telegram in one tap ("Relay to Telegram" skips every screen).
- "Save to Downloads" share target: saves WhatsApp files to Download/FileRelay.
- WhatsApp tab: all files WhatsApp has downloaded (documents, images, videos, voice notes, statuses...).
- File reader: PDF, images, video, audio, text, ZIP contents. Other files open with another app.
- Downloader: paste or share a link to download it.
